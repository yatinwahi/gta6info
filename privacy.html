Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

GTA6 INFO ("the app") runs on its owner's own computer. This policy explains what it does with data from TikTok.

What we get from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your TikTok display name, avatar and account id, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens: issued by TikTok when you connect, used only to post videos you choose to post (video.publish).

Where it is kept

This information is stored only on the computer running the app. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Videos

Clips are made on the owner's computer and uploaded directly to TikTok through TikTok's Content Posting API.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting your TikTok account in the app deletes its stored tokens and profile information. You can also revoke access at any time in your TikTok account settings, under security and permissions.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.

Changes

This policy may change. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through this site's GitHub repository.
