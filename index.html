Short GTA 6 clips from long streams

GTA6 INFO is a personal tool that finds the best moments in long GTA 6 videos and live streams, cuts them into short vertical clips with captions, and posts them to its owner's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The owner picks a video or a live stream to clip.
  2. The software, running on the owner's own computer, transcribes the audio and picks the moments worth sharing.
  3. Each moment is cut to 9:16 with word-by-word captions.
  4. The owner connects their TikTok account with TikTok's own sign-in (Login Kit), and finished clips are uploaded to that account through TikTok's Content Posting API.

What it uses from TikTok

  • user.info.basic: to show which TikTok account is connected.
  • video.publish: to post finished clips to the connected account.

It does not read your messages, followers or other people's data. You can disconnect at any time in the app or in your TikTok settings.

Built with PeakCut, open-source clipping software.
